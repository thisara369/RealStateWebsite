About LandLanka

15+ years of excellence in connecting investors with premium land properties across Sri Lanka. Our journey is built on trust, transparency, and unwavering commitment to our clients.

Our Story

From humble beginnings to becoming Sri Lanka's premier land sales company

Founded in 2008 by visionary entrepreneur Mr. Ranil Perera, LandLanka began as a small real estate consultancy with a big dream: to transform how land transactions are conducted in Sri Lanka.

What started as a one-man operation has grown into a team of 50+ professionals, including real estate experts, legal advisors, market analysts, and customer service specialists.

Our commitment to ethical business practices, transparent transactions, and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of over 5,000 clients worldwide.

Today, we manage a portfolio of 200+ premium properties across Sri Lanka, from beachfront plots in Galle to tea estates in Nuwara Eliya.
